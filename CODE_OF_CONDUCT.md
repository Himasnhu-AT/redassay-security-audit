# Code of conduct

This project is small, and the standard is simple: treat other people the way
you would want to be treated in a code review. Be direct about the work, never
about the person.

## Expected

- Keep criticism technical. Review the patch, the finding, the argument.
- Assume the other person is acting in good faith until you have real evidence
  otherwise.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no, and say it without a long apology.

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer, or open a private security advisory if the report is
sensitive. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. A report
does not need to be perfectly worded to be acted on.

The maintainer decides what happens next, up to and including a permanent ban
from the project. If you disagree with a decision, say so in your reply; it will
be read.
